Membership and event cost calculator

Joining clubs and going to events costs a little money. This calculator shows your bill before you join, so you can plan your budget. Fees are sample data.

How the bill is calculated

Total = (club fees + tickets + T-shirt - free tickets - bundle discount) - senior discount + service fee

  • Club fees are multiplied by 1 for students, 1.5 for staff and 2 for guests.
  • Every 4th ticket is free.
  • Joining 3 or more clubs gives 15% off the club fees.
  • Ages 60 and above get 10% off.
  • Service fee is 5%, but it is free for students who join a club and for ages 60 and above.
