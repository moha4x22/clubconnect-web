About ClubConnect

Our purpose

ClubConnect is a student club management website for Sultan Qaboos University. It gives students, club leaders and Student Affairs one place to find clubs, join them, follow events and share feedback.

Today, news about clubs is spread over posters, social media pages and chat groups, and membership lists are kept in separate spreadsheets. ClubConnect shows how all of this can be gathered in one simple, clear website.

Who is it for?

  • Students who want to find a club that matches their interests.
  • Club leaders who want to manage members and events.
  • The Student Affairs office, which wants a clear view of club activity.

What is new in Part III?

  • The whole site now uses Bootstrap for layout and styling.
  • The club and event tables are built by JavaScript, with search and add forms.
  • New pages: Questionnaire, Calculator and Fun Page.
  • The home page has a scrolling welcome banner and a photo gallery.
