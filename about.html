About ClubConnect

Our purpose

ClubConnect is a website designed to help Sultan Qaboos University students manage and explore student clubs in one place. Students can discover clubs, join them, check upcoming events, and stay updated with the latest announcements.

Currently, club information is shared across different platforms, such as social media, posters, and group chats, while membership details are often stored in separate spreadsheets. ClubConnect aims to make things easier by bringing all this information together in one simple and user-friendly website.

Who is it for?

  • Students who want to find a club that matches their interests.
  • Club leaders who want to manage members and events.
  • The Student Affairs office, which wants a clear view of club activity.
