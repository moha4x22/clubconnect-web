Welcome to ClubConnect

Many students never hear about clubs that match their interests, because the news is spread over posters, social media pages and chat groups. ClubConnect gathers it all in one simple website, so every student can find a club and every club leader can reach students.

New: registration for the November 2026 events is open. Go to the Events page.

What you can do here

  • Discover clubs and search them by name or category.
  • See upcoming events and register for them online.
  • Send a membership request instead of filling in paper forms.
  • Calculate fees, answer the questionnaire and play a game.

How it works

  1. Look at the clubs and the events.
  2. Send a request on the Join a Club page.
  3. Register for events and tell us what you think.
